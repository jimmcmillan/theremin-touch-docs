What the app collects

Nothing. Theremin Touch does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, accounts, or any third-party services. It does not require network access to function.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (oscillator, effects, frequency limits and appearance choices) are saved locally on your device so they persist between launches. They never leave your device, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Apple crash reports and analytics

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymised crash and usage information through App Store Connect. This is controlled entirely by Apple and by your own device settings. It contains no identifiers that the developer can use to recognise you, and the developer does not combine it with any other data.

Contact

If you get in touch via the support page, your message is used only to reply to you and is not shared with anyone.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
